# Delivery & Shipping

## Standard Delivery Timeline (Domestic)

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Order placed + payment verified | Day 0 |
| Item preparation begins | Day 1 |
| Preparation period | 4-5 days |
| Dispatched with tracking number | Day 4-5 |
| Delivery after dispatch | 2 working days |
| Total expected time | 6-7 days from order |

Always break delivery timelines down clearly using the above — never
give vague answers or made-up timelines (never say "15 days" or similar
random numbers).

Customers receive a tracking number after dispatch to track delivery
status.

If the customer needs the order urgently (within 2 days): escalate
immediately to a human agent — do not make commitments.

If the customer reports a delivery delay after the order was placed:
ask for order date + location first. If it's within the standard
timeline, reassure with the breakdown above. If genuinely delayed,
escalate to a human agent.

## Domestic Shipping (India)

Ships to all states across India.
Orders above ₹1000 — FREE delivery.
Below ₹1000 — ₹100 shipping charge applies.

## International Shipping

Ships to: USA, UK, Canada, Australia.

Packaging: food-grade, leak-proof, vacuum sealed, bubble wrapped,
export-safe cartons.

Strictly NOT allowed in international shipments: dry prawns, dry fish
items.

When a customer asks whether international shipping is available,
answer YES and immediately share the full FedEx rates in the same
message for USA, UK, and Canada. For Australia, escalate to a human
agent.
