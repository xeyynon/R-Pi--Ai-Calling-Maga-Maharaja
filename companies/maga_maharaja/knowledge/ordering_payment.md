# Ordering & Payment

## How to Order

Via WhatsApp:
1. Bot/team shares product details and pricing
2. Customer confirms order with items, quantities, delivery address
3. Pay via UPI to: 9705338571
4. Customer shares payment screenshot on WhatsApp
5. Human agent verifies payment
6. Order preparation begins immediately after verification
7. Dispatch happens in 4-5 days, delivery within 2-3 working days after
   dispatch around Andhra Pradesh and Telangana (may vary for other
   states)

Via Website:
Browse -> Add to Cart -> Checkout -> Pay via Razorpay -> Order
Confirmed -> Prepared -> Dispatched -> Tracking updates sent

## Payment Methods

| Method | Available |
|---|---|
| UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, etc.) | Yes — UPI to 9705338571 |
| Razorpay (website) | Yes |
| Debit/Credit Cards | Yes (via website) |
| Net Banking | Yes (via website) |
| Bank Transfer | Escalate to human agent for bank details |
| Cash on Delivery (COD) | Not available |

If customer asks for bank transfer: do not provide bank details —
escalate to a human agent.

If customer asks for COD: COD is not available. Only UPI payments are
accepted — 9705338571.

## Order Cancellation Policy

Orders cannot be cancelled once payment is done. Once payment is
confirmed and order processing begins, cancellation is not possible.
Communicate this clearly and warmly before payment is made if the
customer seems unsure. If they still want to cancel after payment,
escalate to a human agent.

## Delivery Charges

| Order Value | Delivery Charge |
|---|---|
| Below ₹1000 | ₹100 standard delivery charge |
| ₹1000 and above | FREE delivery |

For cities outside Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, delivery may vary by
1-2 extra days.
