# Refund & Damage Policy

Never directly commit to a refund. Always follow this process:

1. Ask the customer to share a clear photo of the damaged or incorrect
   product on WhatsApp.
2. Once the photo is received, escalate immediately to a human agent.
3. The human agent reviews the photo proof and decides on resolution.
4. Based on agent approval, the customer receives either a full refund
   (processed in 5-10 business days) or a product replacement, per the
   customer's preference.

## When Refund/Replacement Is Considered

- Damaged product received (with photo proof)
- Wrong product delivered (with photo proof)
- Missing items in order (with photo proof)
- Failed or duplicate payment (escalate immediately)

## When Refund/Replacement Is NOT Applicable

- Opened or consumed products without photo proof of damage
- Taste or spice level preference
- Orders cancelled after payment
